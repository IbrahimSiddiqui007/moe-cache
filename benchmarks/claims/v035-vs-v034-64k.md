# v0.3.5 reads a 64k-token prompt 38 % faster than v0.3.4 and decodes 10 % slower afterwards

Flash-Next Q2_0 on the Windows PC (Ryzen 9 7950X, RX 9070 XT 16 GB, 32 GB DDR5), both versions on the official llama.cpp b11966 server, cold file cache before each run, PC restarted at 02:03 and the baseline guard passed at 49.4 tok/s. Order new old old new, two runs per cell. Percentages are computed from the means of the two runs (my arithmetic). Source: docs/results-archive/RESULTS.md section 79. Resource metrics (VRAM, process RAM, CPU) and the exact context and -ub per arm were not recorded. At 64k the launcher keeps 39 expert layers on the CPU on a nearly empty card and 40 when other programs hold about 1 GB; v0.3.4 had 37-38. Cause: the bigger prompt chunk (-ub 2048) takes GPU memory, so 2 fewer expert layers fit on the GPU (see decode-room-64k).
